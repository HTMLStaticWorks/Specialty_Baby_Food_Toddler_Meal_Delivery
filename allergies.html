MEDICAL TRANSPARENCY & GUIDELINES

Pediatric Medical Safety & Clinical Allergy Guidelines

We partner with pediatricians and board-certified pediatric allergists to ensure every recipe introduction is transparent, evidence-based, and medically sound.

🩺

Physician-Guided Intro

Before introducing high-risk allergenic proteins (like peanuts, tree nuts, or eggs), consult your pediatrician to confirm your baby's individual readiness.

🧪

Triple ELISA Lab Assays

All equipment undergoes certified protein ELISA swab validation between production runs to guarantee zero cross-contact trace residual.

📋

Allergy Action Protocol

We recommend parents maintain a written Emergency Anaphylaxis Action Plan at home when offering solid food introductions for the first time.

💬

Dietitian Consultation

Our in-house pediatric nutritionists are available to review complex ingredient profiles and answer parent safety questions 5 days a week.
