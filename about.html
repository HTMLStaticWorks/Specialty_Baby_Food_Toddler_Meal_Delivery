OUR KITCHEN STORY

Thoughtful Food Starts in a Thoughtful Kitchen.

We design our meals around simple ingredients, careful preparation, and the practical needs of busy families.

STEP-BY-STEP

Our Kitchen Preparation Journey

From fresh organic harvests to your refrigerator shelf.

01
100% Organic Sourcing

1. Organic Farm Harvest

Direct partnerships with certified organic family farms. Produce is picked fresh within 24 hours of small-batch cooking.

02
Ozonated Rinse

2. Micro-Bubble Prep

Triple cold-water micro-bubble washing removes soil particles and impurities with zero synthetic chemicals.

03
98°C Soft Steam

3. Gentle Steam Cooking

Precision low-temperature steaming retains cell structure, vibrant natural colors, and essential water-soluble B vitamins.

04
Pediatric Textures

4. Milestone Portioning

Mashed, puréed, or diced into exact pediatrician-approved developmental textures tailored for 4–24+ months.

05
Flash Chilling

5. Rapid Sealing & Lab Audit

Flash chilled to 3°C to lock in nutrient density before being sealed in recyclable, 100% BPA-free glass jars.

06
Chilled Delivery

6. Doorstep Fresh Arrival

Shipped weekly in 100% compostable thermal insulation boxes keeping meals fresh for up to 7 days in your fridge.

CORE PRINCIPLES

What Guides Every Meal We Make

We believe feeding your baby should be simple, nutrient-rich, and completely transparent. Every jar and bowl is crafted around four foundational promises to your family:

Simple Ingredients

Clean, single- & multi-origin produce without artificial preservatives, added sodium, or synthetic fillers.

Age-Stage Textures

Specifically adapted purees, velvety mashes, and soft bite-sized cubes tuned to early developmental milestones.

Allergy Control

Rigorous allergen-isolated prep lines, clear package labeling, and customizable parent preference filtering.

Practical Planning

Freshly chilled weekly meal boxes pre-portioned to save busy parents hours of cooking & cleanup each week.
