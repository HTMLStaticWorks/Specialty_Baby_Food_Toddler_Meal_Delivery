PREPARATION & CLINICAL STANDARDS

Food Safety & Quality Standards

Babies have delicate developing microbiomes and immature kidneys. We engineer every kitchen protocol, surface, and cooking cycle around zero contamination and maximum nutrient density.

< 1 ppb Lead Limit

Triple Heavy Metal Screening

Every single harvest lot is independently screened via Inductively Coupled Plasma Mass Spectrometry (ICP-MS) for arsenic, lead, cadmium, and mercury. Our limits are up to 10× stricter than standard grocery thresholds.

  • Zero-tolerance threshold for heavy contaminants
  • Clean Label Project Purity Award certified
  • Certificates of Analysis (COA) archived by lot
99.97% HEPA Filtration

Positive-Pressure Clean Suites

Our cooking and portioning suites operate under positive-pressure medical HEPA air filtration. Air is renewed 24 times per hour to prevent airborne pollen, dust, and environmental particulate contamination.

  • Medical-grade 316 surgical stainless steel
  • Zero Teflon, PFAS, or chemical coatings
  • Contactless sanitization and airlock gowns
Zero Cross-Contact

Validated Allergen Segregation

Dedicated allergen-segregated prep zones ensure allergen-free recipes (dairy, egg, peanut, gluten, soy, fish) are completely isolated. Surfaces undergo high-sensitivity ATP bioluminescence swab validation prior to production.

  • Color-coded allergen-dedicated utensils
  • Bioluminescent ATP protein test verification
  • Custom parent allergen profile auto-filtering
< 90 Min Chill Time

Soft Steam & Rapid Blast Chill

Low-temperature 98°C steam eliminates vegetative bacteria without destroying fragile micronutrients like folate and vitamins B & C. Meals are then flash-chilled to 3°C in under 90 minutes to safely bypass the bacterial growth zone.

  • Zero chemical preservatives or shelf-stabilizers
  • Cellular integrity and natural vibrant color retained
  • Continuous digital temperature telemetry logging
100% Inert Glass

Apothecary-Grade Non-Leaching Jars

We refuse to pack warm baby food in plastic squeezable pouches or aluminum foil linings. Our food is packed exclusively in heavy, non-porous glass jars with vacuum safety lids that guarantee zero microplastics or endocrine disruptors.

  • 100% BPA, BPS, phthalate, and PVC-free
  • Tamper-evident vacuum click safety button
  • Infinitely recyclable and reusable containers
Digital Farm Pedigree

Lot-Level Farm-to-Crib Traceability

Every single jar is laser-etched with an individual lot code. Parents can scan the code to view the exact farm harvest source, harvest timestamp, steam cooking log, and independent laboratory purity assay.

  • Direct farm origin transparency on every delivery
  • Instant batch lookup in Parent Portal dashboard
  • Transparent safety records updated weekly
