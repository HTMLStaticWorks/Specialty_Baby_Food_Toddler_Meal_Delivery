COMMON QUESTIONS

Subscription & Delivery FAQs

Everything parents need to know about billing flexibility, insulated shipping, allergy security, and weekly menu updates.

You are never locked into a strict contract. Inside your Parent Portal, you can skip any upcoming week or pause your subscription with a single click before your weekly lock-in deadline (every Wednesday at midnight EST).

All meals are freshly steamed, flash-chilled to 3°C, and shipped in 100% eco-friendly, compostable insulated boxes packed with non-toxic gel packs. Your food stays safely chilled for up to 48 hours on your doorstep.

Yes! Our allergen management system allows you to select specific allergen exclusions (such as dairy, eggs, peanuts, gluten, or soy). Our kitchen filters out any unsafe recipes automatically from your suggested weekly menu.

Our fresh organic meals last up to 7 days in your refrigerator from the day of delivery. If you wish to store them longer, you can freeze your unopened meal jars for up to 3 months without compromising nutrient density.

Absolutely! The "Family Flow" plan allows you to set up individual child profiles with distinct age stages (e.g., Stage 1 purées for a baby and Stage 4 toddler meals for an older sibling) under a single consolidated account.

Subscriptions are billed weekly on Thursday morning, immediately after the Wednesday midnight menu modification cutoff. You will receive an email receipt and order confirmation with tracking details once shipped.
